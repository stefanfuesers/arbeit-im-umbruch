About us

Hans Meves

Dr. Hans Meves is a sustainability scientist, management consultant, entrepreneur and co-founder of the association Neue Arbeit – Neue Kultur Kiel e.V. (New Work – New Culture Kiel) (NANK). For more than 15 years he has been working intensively on how people, organisations and regions can develop sustainable forms of work and value creation. He is one of the early actors of the New Work movement in northern Germany and connects sustainability, self-determination, social transformation and new cultures of work.

Together with others, he initiated the association in 2010. It follows the ideas of Frithjof Bergmann, the founder of New Work. Bergmann accompanied the founding phase personally and shaped the association's development substantially. NANK aims to support people in unfolding their potential, shaping meaningful work and developing new forms of shared value creation.

In his work, Dr. Meves understands New Work not only as a modern working model but as a cultural change in society. Its core is self-determination, personal responsibility, participation, creativity and sustainable development. His contributions and concepts connect New Work with social innovation, empowerment, regional development and an economy oriented towards the common good.

As founder and managing director of CIVICON | W3-impact, Dr. Meves advises companies, organisations and networks on sustainability strategies, transformation, strategic foresight and new models of value creation. In doing so he bridges the principles of New Work, digital transformation and sustainable development.

His international research and consulting experience from Germany, the USA and Japan has fed into numerous projects, talks and network initiatives. He is particularly committed to building structures that enable people “to do what they really, really want” – a central idea of the philosophy of New Work – New Culture shaped by Frithjof Bergmann.

Stefan Füsers

Stefan Füsers is a facilitator, systemic organisational developer and runs Impact Up. He has been committed to an unconditional basic income for more than two decades. He is a founding member of the European network Unconditional Basic Income Europe (UBIE) and is still active there today.

Professionally, he brings people together to work well with each other. When facilitating workshops, he brings out the cooperative and co-creative potential within a group, also across cultural boundaries.

He is currently training as a systemic organisational developer. He sees great potential in this, because when work is reorganised, organisations have to find themselves anew.

Under Impact Up he runs the Institute for Systemic Impact Orientation. For more than a year he has been exploring there how artificial intelligence can be used for a positive development of society and the world of work.

In Arbeit im Umbruch he connects basic income with New Work. A basic income creates the freedom to answer Frithjof Bergmann's question honestly, what work we really, really want to do.
