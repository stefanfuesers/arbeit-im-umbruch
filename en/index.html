Arbeit im Umbruch

Work is changing faster than it has in generations. Artificial intelligence is taking over tasks that whole professions consisted of only yesterday. Industries are reorganising, and there is much to suggest that the greater part of this change still lies ahead of us.

Whether this change will make us freer is an open question. The sociologist Hartmut Rosa has described what has almost always happened when a new technology was meant to save time. Planes take us around the world faster, and since then we travel far more. An email is quicker to write than a letter, and since then we write far more of them. The time we gained flowed into new tasks, and the pace of life kept rising.

What work do we really, really want to do?

About us

Hans Meves

Co-founder of Neue Arbeit Neue Kultur Kiel e.V. (New Work New Culture Kiel). He built up the project NANK Centres Baltic Sea Region, which connected places of new work in five countries around the Baltic Sea.

Stefan Füsers

Has been committed to basic income for many years, today with Unconditional Basic Income Europe (UBIE). He spent eight years in the Civil Peace Service in Kosovo and Cambodia and works in systemic organisational development.
